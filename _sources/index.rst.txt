===============================================================
Welcome to the Swimmers Documentation
===============================================================

Swimmers is a library for simulating swimming organisms. Three types of swimmers are 
implemented: N-link swimmers, magneto swimmers, and three-sphere swimmers. The first two are flagellated 
microswimmers with magnetic heads.
